Comprehensive Probability and Statistics Questions with Detailed Solutions

Chapter 1: Probability Theory

Question 1

A bag contains:

  • 5 red marbles
  • 3 blue marbles
  • 2 green marbles

One marble is drawn at random.

Solutions:
(a) P(Red) = Number of red marbles / Total marbles = 5/10 = 0.5
(b) P(Not Green) = 1 - P(Green) = 1 - (2/10) = 0.8

Question 2

A fair six-sided die is rolled.

Solutions:
(a) P(>4) = P(5 or 6) = 2/6 = 1/3 ≈ 0.333
(b) P(Even or Prime):

  • Even numbers: 2,4,6 (3 outcomes)
  • Prime numbers: 2,3,5 (3 outcomes)
  • P(Even or Prime) = P(Even) + P(Prime) - P(2) = 3/6 + 3/6 - 1/6 = 5/6 ≈ 0.833

Chapter 2: Discrete Probability Distributions

Question 3

Defective items probability distribution:

X P(X)
0 0.4
1 0.3
2 0.2
3 0.1

Solutions:
(a) Valid because ΣP(X)=1 and all P(X)≥0
(b) μ = Σ[X·P(X)] = (0×0.4)+(1×0.3)+(2×0.2)+(3×0.1) = 1.0
(c)

  • σ² = Σ[(X-μ)²·P(X)] = (0-1)²(0.4)+(1-1)²(0.3)+(2-1)²(0.2)+(3-1)²(0.1) = 1.0
  • σ = √1.0 = 1.0

Question 4

30% prefer tea (p=0.3), sample n=4

Solutions:
(a) Binomial distribution:
P(X=k) = C(4,k)(0.3)k(0.7)(4-k)

(b) P(X≥2) = P(2)+P(3)+P(4) = 6(0.3)²(0.7)² + 4(0.3)³(0.7) + (0.3)^4 ≈ 0.3483

Chapter 3: Binomial Distribution

Question 5

10 questions, 4 options each (p=0.25)

Solutions:
(a) P(X=3) = C(10,3)(0.25)³(0.75)^7 ≈ 0.2503
(b) P(X≥5) = 1 - P(X≤4) ≈ 1 - 0.9219 = 0.0781

Question 6

Defect rate p=0.05, n=20

Solutions:
(a)

  • μ = np = 20×0.05 = 1
  • σ = √(npq) = √(20×0.05×0.95) ≈ 0.9747

(b) P(X≤2) = P(0)+P(1)+P(2) ≈ 0.3585 + 0.3774 + 0.1887 ≈ 0.9246

Chapter 4: Poisson Distribution

Question 7

λ=5 calls/hour

Solutions:
(a) P(X=3) = (e⁻⁵·5³)/3! ≈ 0.1404
(b) For 30 minutes: λ=2.5
P(X≥2) = 1 - P(0) - P(1) ≈ 1 - 0.0821 - 0.2052 ≈ 0.7127

Question 8

λ=2 typos/page

Solutions:
(a) P(X=0) = e⁻² ≈ 0.1353
(b) For 3 pages: λ=6
P(X≥4) = 1 - P(0) - P(1) - P(2) - P(3) ≈ 1 - 0.0025 - 0.0149 - 0.0446 - 0.0892 ≈ 0.8488

Chapter 5: Measures of Central Tendency & Variation

Question 9

Data: 4,6,5,8,7,5,9,6,4,7

Solutions:
(a)

  • Mean = (4+6+5+8+7+5+9+6+4+7)/10 = 6.1
  • Median = 6 (after sorting: 4,4,5,5,6,6,7,7,8,9)
  • Mode = 4,5,6,7 (all appear twice)

(b)

  • Range = 9-4 = 5
  • Variance = Σ(x-μ)²/(n-1) = [(4-6.1)² + (6-6.1)² + ... + (7-6.1)²]/9 ≈ 2.99
  • Standard deviation ≈ √2.99 ≈ 1.73

Question 10

μ=50, σ=10, X=65

Solutions:
(a) z = (X-μ)/σ = (65-50)/10 = 1.5
(b) Interpretation: The value 65 is 1.5 standard deviations above the mean.
